# MYOC ("make your own category") plan

Mock: `.superpowers/brainstorm/99508-1795180704/content/myoc.html`. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-20-myoc-and-nominations-design.md`, restyled with the design system (Century Gothic, dark tokens, pebbles, shared Sheet/ConfirmSheet, one top-right Close X, 44px verb buttons).

## Decisions (from the user)
- Lives in **Settings**, row labelled "MYOC". No Home card (the mock's card is left out for now).
- Scope: member suggestion window, committee review, hand-off to the ballot builder as draft categories (tagged MYOC).
- No nominations feature beyond the existing vote and write-in. No other members' ideas, no upvotes.
- Members see only their own ideas, as "Submitted" or "Withdrawn". No accepted/rejected shown, no notifications, no "idea by X" credit anywhere. The committee sees the submitter.
- Max 5 ideas per member per window (withdrawn ideas free their slot). Window open/close set by the committee. Existing 14 members only.

## Fake Home widget
`components/MakeYourOwnCategories.tsx` ("Created by Jordan") is imported nowhere and is not in `ui/logic/homeWidgets.ts` or the saved-prefs defaults. It is fake and unused, so it is **deleted** (not hidden). No registry or defaults change is needed.

## Not doing
Home card, nominations, upvotes, outcome notifications, "idea by" credit, a "sent to builder" stamp, hard delete.

## Security-sensitive
The migration and its pgTAP test only. Client checks are UX; RLS and the trigger are the enforcement. Known leak to note: a member reading their own row directly through the API can see `accepted`/`rejected`/`merged`; the UI never shows them.
